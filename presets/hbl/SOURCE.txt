splash.png and splash.wav in this folder are derived from the Homebrew Channel's banner
assets by fail0verflow / Team Twiizers:

  https://github.com/fail0verflow/hbc  (commit 13305cfbe5db47eebc5e62aeed14f7adfbb83a5a)
  channel/banner/banner/*.png, channel/banner/sound/wiibrew-banner-*-part.wav
  License: GPL-2.0 (see COPYING in that repository)

Generated with tools/hbc_banner_to_splash.py: one still frame of the animated banner, and the
banner jingle (intro + one pass of the loop) resampled from 32 kHz to 48 kHz.

splash.gif is the banner's real animation (water rising in, the bubble burst, the white flash
and the title), replayed from the keyframes in channel/banner/banner/mkbanner.py by
tools/hbc_banner_to_gif.py: 640x360, 30 fps, 12.6 s (the length of splash.wav, in sync with
it), plays once. It wins over splash.png when both are present. config.ini makes hbmenu
launches show it for 6 s.
